# zerodot brand guidelines

## Essence

- Minimal, self-hosted workflow automation.
- Promise: no telemetry, you own your files.
- Feel: quiet, exact, small. One dot is the start of a flow.

## Name

- Write `zerodot`, lowercase, one word. Start of a sentence: "Zerodot".
- Domain: zerodot.io.

## Voice

- Use short sentences. Use active verbs. Put one idea in each sentence.
- Say what the product does. Do not use hype words ("powerful", "seamless", "revolutionary").
- Tone: calm, direct, a little dry.
- Example: "Self-hosted workflows. No telemetry. Your files."

## Logo

- Mark: one solid orange dot on the left, then three short dashes to the right. The dot is the start. The dashes are the flow.
- Dot color: accent `#FF6900`. Dash color: ink `#0A0A0A`.
- Dashes: thinner than the dot is tall, equal length, equal gap. The dot leads.
- Wordmark: `zerodot`, lowercase, medium weight, tight tracking. Place it to the right of the mark, or below it.
- On dark or accent backgrounds: use white for the dashes and the wordmark. On accent, the dot is white too.
- Clear space: one dot width on all sides.
- Minimum size: 16 px for the mark. Below 24 px, use the dot and one dash only.
- App icon: dot and one dash, on a white, black, or orange tile.
- Do not add gradients, shadows, outlines, or extra shapes. Do not rotate or stretch the logo.

## Color

Use few colors.

| Role   | Value     | Use                             |
| ------ | --------- | ------------------------------- |
| Ink    | `#0A0A0A` | Text, logo                      |
| Gray 1 | `#737373` | Secondary text                  |
| Gray 2 | `#E5E5E5` | Borders                         |
| Paper  | `#FFFFFF` | Background                      |
| Accent | `#FF6900` | Logo dot, links, primary action |
- Use the accent on 5% of a screen at most.
- Use status colors (error, success) only inside the app. Do not use them in brand material.

## Type

- Use one family: Inter or Geist.
- Use one mono family for code and node IDs: Geist Mono or JetBrains Mono.
- Use two sizes: body (14–16 px) and heading (about 1.5× body).
- Make hierarchy with weight first (400 and 600), then with size.
- Use sentence case everywhere. Do not use all caps.

## UI

- Keep it flat. Use 1 px gray borders and a small radius (6–8 px).
- Do not use shadows, except for floating menus.
- Space items on a 4 px grid.
- Icons: one stroke width (1.5 px), outline style.
- Motion: fast (less than 150 ms), no bounce.

## Do and do not

- Do: use much white space, one accent color, and real product screenshots.
- Do not use: stock photos, mascots, 3D, purple-blue gradients, or emoji in UI and docs.
